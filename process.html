From your routine to a plan you can follow

At Mealbeaz, we believe in providing a personalized and comprehensive approach to help you achieve your health and wellness goals. Our step-by-step process is designed to guide you through your journey, ensuring you receive the support and resources needed to succeed. Here's a breakdown of how our process works:

How It Works

A simple step-by-step workflow: We analyze your routine, build your plan, and deliver smart reminders directly to your calendar.

01

Take the Quiz

A short test that asks about your daily schedule, wake-up and bedtimes, work obligations, dietary restrictions, and goals. Takes around 2 minutes.

02

Analyze Your Routine

Our system maps your activity and body rhythms around your day, identifying energy windows, fasts, and ideal moments for optimal metabolic efficiency.

03

Generate Your Plan

Based on your routine, we create a personalized meal and habit plan with suggested timing for meals, hydration, and key nutrients tailored to your schedule.

04

Review and Adjust

Your plan is a suggestion, not a constraint. Swap meals, adjust timing, and tailor recommendations to fit your life before finalizing.

05

Add to Your Calendar

Export your plan to Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, or download an iCal file. Reminders and notifications setup directly within your existing workflow.

06

Get Smart Reminders

Receive notifications at the times you choose: before meals for preparation, or daily check-in points to track progress in real-time.

07

Check In Daily

Mark reminders done, log swapped meals, or indicate if you missed a time. Simple one-tap inputs keep the plan learning your habits.

08

Shop With Less Guesswork

Generate a streamlined grocery list focused on planned meals. Check off what you already have and buy only what's needed.

09

Track Your Progress

Charts show your meal consistency, timing alignment, and trends over time. Clear insights that celebrate small wins.

10

Build Lasting Habits

Over time, Mealbeaz helps you identify what's working and what to adjust. The goal is a sustainable rhythm that fits your routine.
