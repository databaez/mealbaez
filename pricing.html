FAQs

Common questions.

If you don't see your answer here, reach out to our support team.

What's included in the free plan?

The free plan gives you access to the personalized quiz, a basic meal and habit plan, a daily timeline view, and manual checklists. You can save one plan at a time. It's enough to try Rhythm and see if it fits your routine.

Can I change my plan later?

Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time from your account settings. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

How are meal times and suggestions personalized?

Your quiz answers — schedule, food preferences, dietary restrictions, goals, and activity patterns — directly shape your plan. Someone who wakes at 6 AM gets different meal windows than someone who starts at 10 AM. You can adjust everything after the quiz too.

Can I export plans to Google Calendar?

Yes, on Plus and Premium plans. You can export your meal and habit plan as a .ics file that works with Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, and most other calendar apps.

Does it work with dietary preferences and restrictions?

Yes. The quiz asks about dietary restrictions and food preferences, and all meal suggestions respect them. You can update your restrictions anytime in settings.

Is this a replacement for advice from a health professional?

No. Rhythm provides personalized suggestions for meal planning and habit tracking, but it is not medical or therapeutic dietary advice. If you have specific health conditions or therapeutic dietary needs, consult a qualified professional.

Is my personal information shared with other users?

No. Your quiz answers, meal data, and habit history are private by default. Social features on Premium are opt-in, and you choose exactly what to share and with whom.

How do reminders and quiet hours work?

On Plus and Premium, you set when and how often you want nudges — before meals, for habits, or both. Quiet hours let you mute all notifications during sleep, focus time, or weekends.
